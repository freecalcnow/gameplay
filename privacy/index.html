Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Information stored on your device

Arcadia uses browser local storage to remember personal best scores, favourites, recent game activity and simple daily visit streaks. This data stays in your browser unless you clear site data. The current static build does not create user accounts or upload these local records to a server.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This build does not include analytics trackers or active advertising scripts. The site contains clearly marked ad-placement placeholders only. If the site owner later enables analytics or an advertising provider, this policy must be updated to identify the provider, data collected, purposes and controls. Where legally required, a suitable consent mechanism must be used.

Children and families

The site is designed as a general-audience casual game collection. Do not submit personal information through the site. A production launch should include an age-appropriate privacy review and comply with the laws that apply to its audience and location.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details that the site owner adds to the Contact page before publishing.
